Facing suffering and the path to its end
Date: 2026-11-07
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HS5UHPrfc9E&t=73s

Concise summary (reviewed)

Bhante G presents the Four Noble Truths as a practical way to understand suffering and bring it to an end. He traces suffering through birth, aging, sickness, death, and the continuing demands of life. Both frustrated wishes and the fading satisfaction of getting what we want reveal the instability of experience. A story about a house filled with gold illustrates how craving keeps asking for more. He distinguishes desire that perpetuates wanting from the aspiration to become free of desire. The talk concludes with cessation and the Noble Eightfold Path, comparing the four truths to an illness, its cause, its cure, and the way to that cure. He also encourages questions as a support for investigation.
