Q&A: Direct experience and steady practice
Date: 2026-10-25
Type: Q&A

Concise summary (reviewed)

Bhante G answers questions about everyday conduct, Buddhist teachings, and meditation. He recommends limiting habitual television viewing and responding constructively to repeated mistakes within a family. He distinguishes changing mental objects from the enduring truths expressed in the Dhamma and explains knowledge and vision of liberation in relation to arahantship. A question about resuscitation receives a cautious, conditional response that emphasizes consulting medical professionals. Meditation guidance covers returning attention to the nostrils when breathing becomes subtle and observing change directly rather than relying on self-suggestion. The final discussion connects recollection of the Buddha’s qualities and loving-friendliness with confidence, reduced anger, tranquility, equanimity, and the gradual weakening and eventual abandonment of fetters.
