The end of suffering and the path of practice
Date: 2026-09-19
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p9FLPvtQPUc

Concise summary (reviewed)

Suffering can end because it depends on conditions. Bhante G explains the Third Noble Truth as the fading and ending of craving, then turns to the Noble Eightfold Path as the training that makes liberation possible. Everyday relief from anger or desire offers a glimpse of peace, but it is not the complete uprooting of greed, hatred, and delusion. The talk presents all four truths as one practical teaching: understand the problem, recognize its cause, realize its ending, and develop the path. Like medicine, the teaching must be used; knowing or reciting its names is not enough.
