Seeing views of self as conditioned
Date: 2026-10-24
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZ5sLpXGD4c&t=214s

Concise summary (reviewed)

Bhante G explores the Pārileyyaka Sutta (SN 22.81), beginning with background about the monks’ quarrel at Kosambi and the Buddha’s departure into solitude. He then reviews the seven groups making up the 37 aids to awakening before turning to a monk’s question about the destruction of the taints. The central teaching examines views of self as conditioned mental formations. Identification with any of the five aggregates, eternalist and annihilationist views, and uncertainty about the Dhamma are traced through craving, feeling, contact, and ignorance. Bhante G emphasizes that these formations and their conditions are impermanent and dependently arisen. The discourse connects liberation with knowing and seeing this process, rather than merely adopting another position about the self.
