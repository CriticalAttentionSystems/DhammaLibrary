Right Concentration
Date: 2026-09-07
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iwEMYnwoDtU

Concise summary (reviewed)

Right concentration grows from the whole Noble Eightfold Path. Bhante G explains the conditions for the four jhānas, beginning with physical and mental seclusion and freedom from the five hindrances. Their joy is calm and collected rather than excited. Repeated practice develops familiarity with entering, remaining in, leaving, and reviewing these states. As concentration deepens, directed thought gives way to inner confidence, joy becomes subtler, and equanimity and mindfulness mature. The talk ends with a crucial distinction: refined concentration is powerful, but it must be directed toward understanding and the destruction of defilements for the purpose of liberation.
