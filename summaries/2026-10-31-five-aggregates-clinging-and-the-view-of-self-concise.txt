Five aggregates, clinging, and the view of self
Date: 2026-10-31
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SQexjMfJEeQ&t=88s

Concise summary (reviewed)

Bhante G discusses the Full-Moon Night discourse through its questions about the five aggregates: form, feeling, perception, volitional formations, and consciousness. He explains how desire and lust become clinging, including wishes for particular experiences in the future. The discourse examines the conditions for each aggregate and the twenty ways a person can identify the aggregates with a permanent self. It then distinguishes their gratification, their danger, and the escape through abandoning desire and lust. Bhante G emphasizes seeing changing experience clearly without taking it as mine, as what I am, or as myself. A final question about not-self and the effects of actions brings the inquiry back to impermanence and the unsuitability of treating any aggregate as a lasting self.
