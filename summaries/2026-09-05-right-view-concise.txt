Right View
Date: 2026-09-05
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PBe7QlKKIlM

Concise summary (reviewed)

Right view begins with recognizing what harms the mind and what helps it become free. Bhante G explains unwholesome actions and their roots in greed, hatred, and delusion, then the wholesome qualities that oppose them. He expands this investigation through nutriment, the Four Noble Truths, and dependent origination. Again and again, the same questions guide the examination: What is this experience? What causes it? How does it cease? What practice leads to its cessation? Right understanding is more than possessing correct ideas. Its purpose is to weaken deeply rooted attachment, hostility, conceit, and ignorance through the practice of the Noble Eightfold Path.
