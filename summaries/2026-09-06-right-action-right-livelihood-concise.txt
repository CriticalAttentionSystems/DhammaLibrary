Right Action and Right Livelihood
Date: 2026-09-06
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8VivsEv3bo

Concise summary (reviewed)

Our actions and work affect both the mind and the wider community. Bhante G places right action and right livelihood within the whole Noble Eightfold Path, emphasizing intention, conscience, honesty, and the consequences of harm. He discusses killing, stealing, misuse of the senses, harmful trades, and the responsibilities of monastics who depend on others. A difficult example considers livelihood when someone truly has no alternative way to support a family; it is a qualified discussion, not a general approval of harmful work. Earning a fair profit is compatible with practice. Deception and exploitation undermine the peace that meditation requires.
