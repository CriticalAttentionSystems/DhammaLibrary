Q&A: A subtle breath and the practice of letting go
Date: 2026-08-30
Type: Q&A
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MK4-fCkJqS8

Concise summary (reviewed)

What happens to meditation when the breath becomes too subtle to notice? Bhante G suggests observing the changing nature of feelings, thoughts, or consciousness, or returning to the familiar place where the breath was felt. The same insight into impermanence can continue through different objects. Other questions explore learning without becoming attached to ideas, signs of concentration, rebirth, and difficult memories. He repeatedly warns against grasping even peaceful experiences or useful understanding. Study and meditation support one another when teachings are compared with direct experience, while generosity, loving-friendliness, and other wholesome activities help give the mind a beneficial direction.
