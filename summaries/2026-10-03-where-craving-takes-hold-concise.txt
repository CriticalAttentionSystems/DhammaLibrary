Where craving takes hold and how it ceases
Date: 2026-10-03
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=juxwaQdYUjk&t=65s

Concise summary (reviewed)

Where does craving arise, and where does it take hold? Bhante G brings these questions into the experiences of seeing, hearing, and remembering. Observe wanting without guilt, concealment, or blindly following it. He explains that craving becoming quiet is not the same as its complete cessation: its roots may remain and become active again. The talk then applies contemplation of impermanence and not-self to the eye and ear, including their objects, consciousness, feelings, and the thoughts and desires built around them. Repeated, careful observation prepares the way for disenchantment, dispassion, and freedom from attachment. The other sense doors follow the same pattern.
