The four sights and the causes of suffering
Date: 2026-09-12
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CF2UniVfk5c

Concise summary (reviewed)

Old age, sickness, and death reveal the limits of ordinary security; the sight of a renunciant suggests another way to live. Bhante G follows the Buddha's search from these four sights through the rejection of indulgence and self-mortification to the discovery of dependent origination. Suffering depends on causes, so it can end when those causes are removed. The teaching becomes immediate when we notice craving for pleasant experiences, resistance to unpleasant ones, and restless searching when life feels dull. Letting go does not mean abandoning family or kindness. It means caring without demanding permanent possession or control.
