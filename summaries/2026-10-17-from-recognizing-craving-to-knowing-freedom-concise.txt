From recognizing craving to knowing freedom
Date: 2026-10-17
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1m81GDboT-g&t=296s

Concise summary (reviewed)

Bhante G concludes his teaching on the Four Foundations of Mindfulness by showing how recognizing craving becomes a practice of release. Identify the sense door and the part of experience that supports wanting, then contemplate its impermanence, dependence on conditions, and absence of a permanent self. Repeating the contemplation helps only when it directs clear observation. Practice both when craving appears and before it becomes strong; repeated difficulties are opportunities to learn. Bhante G distinguishes passing calm from liberation and the understanding that knows why freedom has occurred. He connects the completion of practice with the four tasks of understanding suffering, abandoning its origin, realizing cessation, and developing the path.
