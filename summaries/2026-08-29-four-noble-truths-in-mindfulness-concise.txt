The Four Noble Truths in mindfulness practice
Date: 2026-08-29
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xrUXWEw-rnY

Concise summary (reviewed)

The Four Noble Truths are the heart of mindfulness practice, not a separate set of beliefs. Bhante G explains how observing body and mind prepares us to understand suffering, its cause, its ending, and the path. Suffering includes obvious pain and loss, but also the recurring feeling that happiness is still somewhere ahead. This does not deny friendship, beauty, or joy. It asks whether changing experiences can provide permanent security. Through the Buddha's search for true happiness, the talk encourages an honest investigation of craving and clinging, followed by practice rather than the mere repetition of teachings.
