Concise summary (reviewed)

How can concentration become part of an ordinary, busy day? Bhante G recommends a regular morning sitting and brief pauses to return to the breath during work. He also explains how breath counting can steady a wandering mind, how loving-friendliness supports concentration, and why concentration must be used to investigate impermanence rather than treated as complete liberation. Other questions address hearing in jhāna, the rapid sequence of feeling and perception, and the meaning of becoming. Throughout the session, he brings attention back to training the mind: recognize the pain caused by greed, hatred, and confusion, then patiently cultivate their wholesome alternatives.
