Q&A: Recognizing progress and working with the mind
Date: 2026-10-11
Type: Q&A

Concise summary (reviewed)

This morning Q&A explores how to recognize meditation progress through greater patience, compassion, steadier attention, and less anger. Bhante G answers questions about the fourth jhana and the divine eye, observing impermanence, memory and not-self, and moving through the four groups of mindfulness-of-breathing exercises. He explains that awareness may move naturally between the breath at the nose and the body's expansion and contraction, and that seeing a bright light is not required for progress. Other answers address discouragement, self-criticism, expectations of others, generosity, and difficulty sustaining concentration. He encourages consistent daily practice and observing change even when concentration remains weak. Loving-friendliness is recommended when “darkness” means sadness or a troubled emotional state.
