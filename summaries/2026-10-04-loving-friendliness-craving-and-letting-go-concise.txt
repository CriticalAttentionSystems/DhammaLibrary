Q&A: Loving-friendliness, craving, and letting go
Date: 2026-10-04
Type: Q&A
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ndMmorYNMc&t=138s

Concise summary (reviewed)

How can loving-friendliness help when meditation brings tension and aversion? Bhante G recommends beginning with mettā and observing ill will mindfully when it arises. Other questions explore craving for existence, honesty expressed kindly, and the meaning of change and cessation. He affirms that eating well and exercising are good while encouraging the reduction of craving. Mindfulness of breathing develops dispassion and letting go; he distinguishes the ordinary passing of experience from cessation without renewed arising. He also encourages interest in a relaxed, slow breath and explains how boundless loving-friendliness includes unseen beings whose lives and work support our everyday experience.
