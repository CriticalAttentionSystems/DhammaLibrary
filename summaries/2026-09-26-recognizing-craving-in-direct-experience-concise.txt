Recognizing craving in direct experience
Date: 2026-09-26
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y7LBj5sIy9o

Concise summary (reviewed)

Knowing that craving causes suffering is only a beginning. Bhante G asks meditators to recognize craving where it actually arises: in seeing, hearing, bodily feeling, memories, opinions, and other experiences. Rather than feeling guilty or following the desire, observe its conditions and changing nature. A pleasant sound or remembered experience need not become a whole story of wanting and resistance. The talk also distinguishes temporary quiet from the abandonment of craving: a desire may subside while its underlying tendency remains. Mindfulness and clear comprehension make that process visible, preparing the way for wisdom to weaken attachment.
