Q&A: Investigation, effort, and freedom from clinging
Date: 2026-09-20
Type: Q&A
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6wfrum1ZcQ

Concise summary (reviewed)

Bhante G distinguishes investigating experience from merely thinking about it. Mindfulness examines impermanence, unsatisfactoriness, and the absence of a permanent self in body and mind. Questions then explore sitting practice, restraint of the senses, awakening, and the value of studying different Buddhist traditions. His recurring emphasis is to understand the Four Noble Truths in one's own life. Trying to force the mind blank is not the aim: cultivate wholesome qualities through the four right efforts. The session closes by relating fear to attachment—the desire to keep pleasure, health, and youth brings anxiety about losing them.
