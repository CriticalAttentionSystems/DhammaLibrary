Q&A: Self-kindness, not-self, and a calmer mind
Date: 2026-10-18
Type: Q&A
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UBfU28bB4vk&t=312s

Concise summary (reviewed)

This Q&A explores sleeplessness, identification with “I” and “mine,” recurring memories, and kindness toward oneself. Bhante G suggests gentle attention to breathing or loving-friendliness when anxiety and overthinking make sleep difficult. He describes how observing conditioned, impermanent experience can gradually weaken self-identification, and explains that memory does not necessarily depend on craving or clinging. Further answers distinguish disenchantment from dispassion and describe investigation of mental states and their causes. For self-criticism, emotional difficulty, and tension during concentration, he emphasizes relaxation, patient mettā practice, and understanding experience without punishing oneself. He also recommends discourses supporting contemplation of impermanence and not-self, and returns to loving-friendliness as a practice for aversion.
