Insight at the sense doors: seeing clearly and letting go
Date: 2026-10-10
Type: Dhamma talk
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vkCGcLHCi5g&t=58s

Concise summary (reviewed)

Bhante G explains how insight into the senses can loosen craving and clinging. Using smell as a worked example, he distinguishes the smelling faculty, its object, consciousness, contact, and the experiences that follow. Each is to be observed as impermanent, conditioned, and not-self. This is practical investigation supported by moral conduct, learning, and regular meditation, not simply repeating a formula. Seeing clearly leads to disenchantment, the fading of passion, and freedom from grasping. Disenchantment does not mean depression or hatred, and freedom is more than a passing calm. The same investigation applies to the other senses and requires continued, diligent practice.
