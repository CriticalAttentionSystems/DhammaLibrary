ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

The teaching comes first.

This library brings together Bhante Gunaratana’s Dhamma talks and questions and answers from the Bhavana Society YouTube channel. Each entry connects a concise overview with a longer summary and the original recording.

Summaries are prepared with AI assistance and edited against the transcript. They are reading aids; the linked recording remains the source. Pali classes and closing recitations are outside this collection.

Curated by Veronique. For a correction, include the video link and relevant time in your message to criticalattentionsystems@gmail.com.
